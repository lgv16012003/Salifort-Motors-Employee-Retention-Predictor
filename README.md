# Salifort Motors Employee Retention Predictor

## 🔹 Project Overview
The purpose of this project is to analyze employee operational metrics at Salifort Motors and build a predictive classification model to understand the core drivers behind employee turnover. Currently, high voluntary resignation rates are creating significant financial strain due to recruitment and training overheads. By uncovering these underlying workforce patterns, senior leadership can transition from reactive management to proactive retention strategies.

## 🔸 Objective
The core goal is to build an advanced machine learning classification framework that accurately predicts whether an employee is likely to stay or leave the corporation based on workload, evaluations, satisfaction levels, and compensation tiers. In doing so, we aim to identify operational pain points, quantify feature importance weights, and deliver practical, metrics-driven structural recommendations to improve overall employee retention and long-term job satisfaction.

## 🟨 Key Results & Analysis
- **Model Performance:** The Random Forest champion model achieved an outstanding **95.2% Overall Accuracy** and a high **93.1% F1-score** for the turnover class, successfully identifying at-risk employees while maintaining a very low false-alarm rate.
- **Top Turnover Drivers:** Workload intensity and corporate stagnation are the strongest statistical predictors of departure. Employees who work extreme hours (**>240 average monthly hours**) and handle **6-7 major projects** experience severe burnout. 
- **Tenure Ceiling:** A massive cluster of resignations occurs among employees reaching the **3-to-5-year tenure milestone**. Surprisingly, these departures consist of high-performing individuals with excellent evaluation scores who choose to leave due to a lack of promotions or salary adjustments.

## 🟩 Strategic Next Steps
1. **Cap Project Workloads:** Limit employees to a maximum of **4–5 projects** simultaneously to prevent workload burnout.
2. **HR Wellness Flags:** Implement automated system alerts for any employee working over **220 hours/month** to trigger immediate workload reviews.
3. **Overhaul Promotions:** Review and adjust compensation and promotion structures specifically for high performers in the **3–5 year tenure bracket** to boost retention.
